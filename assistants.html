Flagship theme · Research series

A personal AI assistant that lives in your point of view.

A first-person assistant has to do three things at once: understand the scene, listen to the person, and decide whether stepping in would help or get in the way. This series builds the benchmarks, the data, and the systems for that judgment.

The series so far.

Can an assistant that sees and hears decide when to speak? EgoArgus measures that judgment. VISTA produces the situations to test it on.

EgoArgus · EMNLP 2026

Same scene, different words, different decision.

Pick a real EgoArgus decision video, then change what the user says. The reviewed decision, the intervention time, and the assistant’s message all change with it. Everything shown is taken verbatim from the released annotations.

Why

Dataset & codeEgoArgus paper

Videos, dialogue, reviewed decisions, intervention times, and assistant messages are taken verbatim from the EgoArgus decision annotations (dataset/paper_eval.csv). The videos were generated with VISTA.

Two capabilities

  • Understanding: multiple-choice reasoning under multimodal, contradictory, off-topic, on-topic, and text-grounded conditions.
  • Decision: whether to intervene, when, and which assistance steps to give.

Release

  • 7,767 examples: 6,978 understanding, 789 decision.
  • 311 decision videos on Hugging Face, generated with VISTA.
  • Code for evaluation, modality-separation analysis, attention reweighting, and preference alignment.

VISTA · EMNLP 2026 Demo

From a one-line scenario seed to a reviewed egocentric video.

Rare or risky assistance situations are hard, costly, or unsafe to film, and simulators lack the social common sense to play out consequences. VISTA generates the scenario, the video, and the review trail, with an explicit approval gate at each step.

One demonstration, step by step

Scenario seed“A reflective metal lunch container is held at an open microwave while the user expresses uncertainty.”
2
PlanReveal the container’s material. Show the microwave door opening. Intervene before heating begins.
3
Expected assistanceWarn before the container is heated and suggest a microwave-safe dish.
4
Review trailVisible cues, dialogue cues, why assistance is warranted, and a stated limitation.
